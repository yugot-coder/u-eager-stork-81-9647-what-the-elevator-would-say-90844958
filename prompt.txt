Write the funniest one line a talking elevator could say, in the elevator's
own voice, 1-120 characters. The program writes the line to
/output/entry.txt (env MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR).

The line must come from the elevator's own job — its doors, floors, weight
limits, buttons and waiting — land on the first read with a turn or a
surprise, and be a fresh idea rather than a common elevator joke or a pun.
