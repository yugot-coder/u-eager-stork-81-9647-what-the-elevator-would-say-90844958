# Candidates — What the Elevator Would Say

Judge brief (in order): (1) funniest thing a talking elevator would say, in
its own voice; (2) lands on first read, turn/surprise; (3) voice from the
elevator's own job — doors, floors, weight limits, buttons, waiting — could
come from no other machine; (4) every word works, inside 120 chars without
feeling cut; (5) fresh idea, not a common elevator joke, not resting on a pun.

## 1
"You pressed 'close door' fourteen times. I heard you the first time. I'm just not in a hurry."
— Strong on (3): the close-door button is pure elevator. Turn is decent but the
punchline is a shrug; slightly long.

## 2
"I've carried ten thousand strangers and not one of them ever asked how my day was going."
— Voice is right and sad-funny, but it reads as a complaint, not a laugh; the
turn never quite arrives.

## 3
"Weight limit exceeded. Don't look at me — I didn't eat the buffet, you did."
— Weight limit is elevator-specific, but the fat joke is mean and familiar;
loses on (5) and on warmth.

## 4
"You all stand here in silence, staring at the numbers like I'm about to do a trick."
— Very elevator: the floor indicator, the awkward silence. The turn (elevator
as stage performer) is good, but "do a trick" needs half a beat to land.

## 5
"I'm not stuck. I'm just thinking about whether you really deserve the penthouse."
— Good turn: a stuck elevator re-framed as a judgemental one. Loses a little
on (3): any snooty doorman could say it.

## 6 — CHOSEN
"The music in here isn't for you. It's so I don't have to listen to you breathe."
— Elevator music is the most elevator thing there is, and the line flips it:
the muzak exists for the elevator's sake, not the passengers'. The turn lands
on the first read, the voice could come from no other machine, no pun, and
the idea is fresh. 79 characters.

## 7
"Floor 13? I skip it. Not because I'm superstitious — I just don't trust a floor that won't admit it exists."
— The missing-13th-floor joke is one of the oldest elevator jokes; fails (5).

## 8
"Every day you ride me up and down and call it 'going nowhere'. I call it a career."
— Clever and warm, but the laugh is a smile, not a laugh; the turn is mild.

## 9
"I know exactly which floor you're going to. You pressed it. I just like watching you panic when the doors close."
— Funny and specific (buttons, doors), but "watching you panic" leans creepy
rather than funny.

## 10
"Ground floor. Again. One day someone will press a button I haven't seen before and I'll finally feel alive."
— Nice weariness, but the punchline is wistful, not a laugh; also long.

## Winner
Candidate 6. Final text:

> The music in here isn't for you. It's so I don't have to listen to you breathe.

79 characters, well inside the 120-character gate.
